DMCA Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

MyKK is a browser start page in two parts: the MyKK Cloud Dashboard at start.mykk.us, and the MyKK.us browser extension that activates Pro features for subscribers. What you create in the dashboard — bookmarks, notes, widget layout, and theme — is kept in your own browser's local storage; if you turn on cloud sync, that configuration is stored in your own Google Firebase account rather than on our systems. Our servers hold account, licence, subscription, and device-activation records only, and never the content you save. This policy covers this website (mykk.us), the dashboard, and any material you submit to us directly. We respect the rights of copyright holders and respond to valid notices under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. §512.

Reporting alleged infringement

If you believe material on this website, or material you have submitted to us, infringes a copyright you own or represent, send a written notice to our designated agent (below) that includes:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough information for us to locate it (for example, the page URL on mykk.us).
  4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on its behalf.

Designated agent

DMCA Agent, MyKK — send your notice through our contact form with “DMCA Notice” as the subject. We review complete notices promptly and remove or disable access to infringing material where the notice is valid.

Counter-notification

If you believe material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification through the same contact form including: your signature; identification of the material and the location where it appeared before removal; a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification; and your name, address, and telephone number, with consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate federal court and to accept service of process from the complaining party.

Repeat infringers

We maintain a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, access for users who are repeat infringers.

Not legal advice

This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. Misrepresenting that material is infringing may expose you to liability under 17 U.S.C. §512(f).
